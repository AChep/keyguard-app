Keyboard shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+Space to autofill instantly. With a single match the credentials are filled directly; with multiple matches the popup opens for selection.

Firefox does not let extensions open the shortcut editor directly. To remap it, open the Add-ons Manager (about:addons), click Settings (⚙) → Manage extension shortcuts, and find Keyguard Autofill.
