Keyguard fills your logins in other apps and in browsers through the system
autofill service. Suggestions match the app or website you're on, and can
appear in your keyboard.

<Screenshot src={autofillKeyboard} caption="An inline autofill suggestion in the keyboard above a login form" />

When nothing matches, you can search the vault instead. After you fill a
login, Keyguard can copy its one-time password, ready to paste.
