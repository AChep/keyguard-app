Pull your vault out on demand, whether that means a handful of individual items or an entire organization's collection. Exports can optionally include the attachments.

<Screenshot src={exportAccount} caption="An account's export options, for individual items or the entire vault." />

Move to another tool, archive a copy for safekeeping, or hand off an organization's data when ownership changes. Your credentials, notes, and the files attached to them leave in full.

If you are moving to another password manager app on the same Android device, [credential exchange](/features/credential-exchange/) can hand one account's items over directly, without the attachments, and only in builds distributed with Google Play services. See its [requirements](/docs/credential-exchange/#requirements).
