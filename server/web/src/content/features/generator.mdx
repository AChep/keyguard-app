The generator creates a password, a passphrase from the built-in or your own
wordlist, a PIN, a username, an SSH key or a GPG key. Passwords and passphrases
show their strength as you change the options.

<Screenshot src={genPassphrase} caption="Generating a passphrase from the EFF long wordlist, with word count and separator controls." />

Email aliases give every signup its own address, so spam or a leak traces back
to one service. Aliases come from a forwarding service or from an inbox you
already own.

<Screenshot src={genCatchEmail} caption="Generating a catch-all email address on a domain you own." />
