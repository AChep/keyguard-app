Keep all your vaults in one place. Connect Bitwarden accounts on any server,
including Vaultwarden, and KeePass databases side by side, and see their items
in one list.

Search covers every account at once, and a filter narrows the list to one
account. Give each account its own name and color, hide the ones you rarely
use, and copy items between accounts.
