A local copy of your vault stays on your device, so every item is readable with or without a connection.

You can keep working offline too: add items and Bitwarden Sends, edit existing ones, and attach files. The changes are held locally until you have a connection again.
Once you are back online, your changes sync the next time you open the vault. If something was edited in more than one place, the conflicts are resolved using a [3-way merge](/docs/sync-and-conflicts/).
