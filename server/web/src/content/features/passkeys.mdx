Keyguard creates passkeys and signs you in with them as your device's passkey
provider. A passkey is bound to the site that issued it, so a look-alike page
has no password to collect.

<Screenshot src={passkeyCreate} caption="Creating a passkey for a website during account sign-up" />

Each passkey lives in your vault next to your logins and syncs with them.
Watchtower points out sites that support passkeys where you haven't created
one yet.

<Screenshot src={passkeyItem} caption="A passkey item stored in the vault next to other login entries" />
