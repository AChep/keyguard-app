Search matches the fields inside each item, not just its title, so a username, an email address, or a fragment of a website is enough to find the right one. Results update as you type, across a vault of thousands of entries.

When several results look alike, narrow them down. You can refine by item type, folder, or tags to cut a broad list into a precise one without leaving the search field.

<Screenshot src={searchFields} caption="A partial query surfaces matches instantly, highlighted across titles, fields, and attachments." />

Qualifiers narrow it further. `attachment:` matches items by the names of the files attached to them, and the type and tag filters stack on top.

<Screenshot src={searchAttachment} caption="The attachment: qualifier narrows results to items with a matching file, alongside the type and tag filters." />
