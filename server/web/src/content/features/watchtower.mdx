Watchtower audits every item in your vault and tells you what needs attention:
leaked, reused and weak passwords, breached sites, weak keys, and websites
still on plain HTTP. It also finds clutter, such as duplicates, incomplete
items and expiring cards.

<Screenshot src={watchtowerOverview} caption="The Watchtower overview, with a password-strength summary and security issues grouped by category with counts." />

Each finding opens the affected items, so you can change a password, fix a URL
or merge duplicates on the spot. The checks that look up breaches never send
your passwords anywhere.
